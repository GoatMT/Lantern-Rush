      TOUCH CONTROLS

      Hold Shoot to charge; release to strike. While charging, swipe Sprint & Skill upward or hold its upper edge for a curved shot. You can also slide your shooting thumb upward. Foot and body position choose Finesse or Trivela. A full bar is risky, especially at speed or from a tight angle.

      Sprint & Skill: hold for speed, swipe up without charging for a skill. Dribble performs a contextual move; Dribble while charging fakes the shot. PASS follows team possession; SWITCH selects a nearby outfield player. Steals are automatic when you move close enough to challenge the ball. GOALIE toggles keeper control. Hold SWITCH and drag up, down or left to select your keeper.

      KEYBOARD

      Select a key, then press its replacement. Bindings swap automatically. M passes or switches to a nearby outfield player. Standing steals happen automatically; G toggles goalkeeper control. Hold keyboard Pass for pressure or a first-time return. Hold Shift with Shoot for a curved shot.
